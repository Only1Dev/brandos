[AUREON TECHNOLOGIES - PRODUCT LAUNCH ANNOUNCEMENT]

Headline: Next-Gen Transaction Security Powered by Aureon Shield Intelligence

Body:
Aureon Technologies today announced expanded deployment of its enterprise risk defense architecture, Aureon Shield Intelligence. Designed specifically for Tier-1 banking partners and high-throughput financial institutions, the platform provides automated risk defense, SOC2 compliance governance, and audited protection against novel payment vector fraud.

In recent independent benchmark evaluations, institutions deploying Aureon Shield demonstrated an audited 42% average reduction in transaction fraud while maintaining ultra-low false positive interruption rates.

"Modern financial infrastructure requires uncompromising precision and provable governance," stated the engineering lead at Aureon. "With autonomous anomaly detection and real-time ledger verification, we enable partners to protect depositor assets without compromising settlement velocity."

Availability & Compliance:
Aureon Shield Intelligence is immediately available for enterprise banking environments. Full SOC2 Type II audit attestations, encryption specifications, and integration documentation can be accessed through verified partner portals.
